Privacy Policy

Kan News Social Media Analytics Dashboard

Last updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

This privacy policy describes how the Kan News Analytics Dashboard collects, uses, and protects data obtained from social media platforms.

2. Data We Collect

The application collects the following types of data from connected social media accounts:

  • Public engagement metrics (views, likes, shares, comments)
  • Follower and subscriber counts
  • Content performance statistics
  • Aggregate audience demographics (when available)

3. How We Use Data

All collected data is used exclusively for:

  • Internal performance reporting
  • Content strategy optimization
  • Editorial decision-making support

4. Data Storage

Data is stored securely and accessed only by authorized Kan News personnel. We do not sell or share data with third parties.

5. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact the Kan News digital team.
